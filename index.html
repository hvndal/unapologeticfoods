Chapter one · the house rule

We don’t make it milder.

Unapologetic Foods cooks Indian food the way it is eaten at home, on the street and at weddings across the subcontinent. Region by region. Spice by spice. Nothing softened for anybody.

That’s how Dhamaka, Semma and Adda earned their following in New York. Adda Philly brings the canteen classics to Fishtown, across from the post office, with an open kitchen, a grill, and a room built for staying too long.

Chapter three · below the dining room

नीचेDownstairs

A small bar and a kitchen counter under the dining room, for whatever the chefs can’t stop thinking about.

Chef-driven dinners, one-night projects and the drinks that go with them. It changes often and seats very few. Ask for it when you book.

Mumbai and Kolkata walk into Fishtown.

They built the most talked-about Indian restaurants in America by refusing the one thing everyone told them to do: tone it down.

No butter-chicken-for-beginners. No “mild, medium, hot.” Just the food of specific places, cooked by people who grew up eating it. Philly gets the same deal.
