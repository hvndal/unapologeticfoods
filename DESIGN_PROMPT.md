# Design brief v2 — Adda Philly · Unapologetic Foods

Rebuild the Adda Philly pitch site (index.html / style.css / app.js) as a site worthy of a
Michelin-level restaurant group. v1 was a loud 70s-poster exercise; v2 keeps the Indian
identity but carries it with restraint, craft and the dishes themselves.

## Who and where
Adda Philly, 1700 Frankford Ave, Fishtown, Philadelphia. By Unapologetic Foods (Chef Chintan
Pandya and Roni Mazumdar: Dhamaka, Semma — Michelin star, NYT No. 1 2025 — Adda, Masalawala &
Sons, Kebabwala, Rowdy Rooster). Their stance: Indian food on its own terms, nothing softened.
The audience is Philadelphia diners, so English leads; Devanagari appears only as a quiet
bilingual second voice (dish names, a signature), never as display type.

## The idea
**Pink City at dusk, lit by fire.**
- The fire (hero video) is the hearth: dimmed, graded warm, embers in darkness. The logo sits on it.
- Jaipur's palace walls (peacock-feather petals in blush and sage, cusped arches, gilded hairlines)
  become the ornament system: fine-line, low-contrast, used like a watermark or a frame.
- Kalamkari / "Strokes of India" editorial: oxblood grounds, pale-blush serif, hand-drawn line.
- Unapologetic's red dot (from their wordmark) is the one hard accent: brand punctuation.

## References for the level
Semma, Gymkhana London, Indian Accent, Atomix, Eleven Madison Park, Noma: full-bleed food,
generous space, one confident serif, reservations one tap away, menus set like print.

## System
- **Palette:** ink #15100e, oxblood #5a1f1a (deep #3a1411), bone #f2e9dd, blush #e6bcb2,
  palace green #22362d, sage #8ea08c, antique gold hairlines #b8925a, red dot #c8102e.
  One ground colour per section; accents sparing.
- **Type:** Fraunces (light, high optical size, soft) for display and statements, italic for
  the emphasised word; Jost for labels/UI in tracked small caps and for body text;
  Tiro Devanagari Hindi for the small bilingual line. Chapter labels in Roman numerals.
- **Ornament:** peacock-petal pattern, cusped-arch frames, jaali lattice, lotus divider — all
  1px gold/blush lines, never loud. No gradients-as-decoration, no glass, no pill badges.
- **Imagery:** real food photography, graded to one warm, low-saturation look; framed in
  arches or run full-bleed. Hearth craft shown as gold line drawings (tandoor, tawa, dum handi).
- **Motion:** slow and few: hero video, line-by-line text rise, images unmasking upward,
  a cursor-following dish preview on the menu (desktop). Respects reduced motion.

## Sections
Hero (fire + logo) · I The House · II The Signature (Butter Chicken Experience) · III The Menu
(featured plates + full menu, dish detail) · IV The Hearth (tandoor, tawa, dum) ·
V Downstairs · VI Unapologetic (accolades + the family) · VII Visit (address, newsletter,
footer). Reservation: a Resy-quality panel (party, 14-day date strip, times, preorder,
confirmation with calendar file). Presenter bar for the 12 pitch screens + phone preview.

## Done means
Every section, both modals and the phone preview look designed at 1440, 768 and 390px; no
horizontal scroll; mobile has a sticky reserve bar; accessible focus states and contrast.
